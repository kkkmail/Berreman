Here are some comments. DispersionExpression must be elevated for eps , mu, and rho, e.g. EpsDispersionExpression 
so that to never mix them up. This apply to all similar things: e.g. a wavelength interval is just an interval, 
but a dispersion expression is always specific to something.
Some dispersive models are explicitly real and have separate formulas for n and k. We must keep it that way as 
using complex numbers when the are NOT needed results in extra complexity. WaveLengthInterval is fixed for uniaxial 
and biaxial media, which means that MediumDispersion is NOT correctly structured. This means that we need 3 flavors 
of DispersionSegment (for isotropic, uniaxial and biaxial media) and then we elevate that further into a collection. 
Note that validity is a bad label because it does not say what that validity is. Since a media may have some or all 
dispersive or non-dispersive properties, then the correct model a DU option tree: all media have eps it could be 
dispersive on non-dispersive. That’s two DU cases. The media may have magnetic and optical activity properties. 
That’s two options and under the hood each of them is similar two case DU as for eps. Note, that all of these things 
MUST be elevated to their `Eps<Something>`, `Mu<Something>`, `Rho<Something>`, so that they can never be mixed.
